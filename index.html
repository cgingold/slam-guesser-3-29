🎾Slam Guesser

How to Play

Each puzzle shows a tennis pro's Grand Slam results — your job is to guess who it is.

You get three tries; each wrong guess reveals a hint (gender, then nationality, then the answer).

Use the mode toggle to play Standard (any pro), Winners (slam champions only), or Hard (years hidden).
